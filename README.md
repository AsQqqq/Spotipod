## Overview

What if you could bring back the iconic iPod experience without giving up your Spotify library?

**SpotiPod** is a modern reimagination of the classic iPod, bringing its legendary interface, familiar navigation, and nostalgic atmosphere to your desktop.

It combines the timeless design of Apple's iconic music player with the convenience of Spotify, turning everyday music listening into something a little more special.

This isn't just another music player skin. It's an attempt to recreate the feeling of holding and using an actual iPod.

## Features

- **Classic iPod Interface** — A faithful recreation of the iconic iPod interface and its visual identity.
- **Click Wheel Navigation** — Navigate through menus using the legendary wheel-inspired controls.
- **Authentic Sound Effects** — Familiar Click Wheel sounds accompany navigation, recreating the original tactile experience.
- **Spotify Integration** — Control Spotify playback and interact with your music library.
- **Nostalgic Experience** — Rediscover the charm of the 2000s through a carefully recreated interface.
- **Modern Convenience** — Enjoy the aesthetics of a classic device while keeping your modern music streaming workflow.

## Behind the Project

### A Random Discovery

Every project has a story. SpotiPod started with something rather unexpected: a 404 error page on Apple's official music website, [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com/).

The page featured a classic iPod. For someone fascinated by Apple's ecosystem, it was more than just an error page — it was a spark of inspiration.

The idea was simple: bring that iconic device back to life, this time on a computer screen.

### More Than Just a Skin

SpotiPod isn't simply a visual imitation. Its virtual firmware and interface are designed to recreate the original iPod experience as closely as possible.

From the familiar menu structure to the characteristic Click Wheel sound effects, attention has been paid to the little details that made the original device so memorable.

The goal is not just to make something that looks like an iPod, but something that feels like one.

### Built to the Soundtrack of the 2000s

There was a particular atmosphere behind the development process.

To get into the right mindset, the entire project was developed while listening exclusively to music from the 2000s — the very era when owning an iPod was a dream for so many people.

Perhaps that's why nostalgia became such an important part of SpotiPod's identity.

### A Different Way to Experience Spotify

SpotiPod does not attempt to bypass Spotify's restrictions or capture its audio streams.

Due to Spotify's platform limitations, the application works as a custom remote control for the official Spotify client.

Your music stays where it belongs — inside Spotify. SpotiPod simply gives you a different way to interact with it.

### The Nostalgia Effect

Using SpotiPod instead of the usual Spotify window tends to get people's attention.

For those who remember the original iPod era, navigating through albums and switching tracks with a virtual Click Wheel can feel like stepping back in time.

And for everyone else, it's a chance to experience a small piece of Apple's design history.

## A Little iPod History

The original iPod was introduced by Apple on October 23, 2001, with the promise of putting 1,000 songs in your pocket.

It became one of the most recognizable consumer electronics devices in history and helped shape the future of portable music.

A few interesting facts:

- **1,000 Songs in Your Pocket** — The first iPod featured a 5 GB hard drive and could store approximately 1,000 songs.
- **The Click Wheel** — Its distinctive navigation system became one of the device's most recognizable design elements.
- **A Revolution in Music** — The iPod helped popularize digital music libraries and transformed how people carried and listened to music.
- **The Road to iPhone** — The iPod helped establish design and software ideas that influenced Apple's later products, including the iPhone.
- **The End of an Era** — Apple discontinued the final iPod model, the iPod touch, in May 2022.

The iPod may be gone, but its design legacy remains.
